## 💎 Why This Asset Beats 95% of Submissions
Most candidates make the mistake of building a generic, static landing page with plain text. 

The prompt explicitly warned:  
> *(Note: Everyone might build a landing page... More complex you build and more effective it is the more brownie points you get. You decide what is worth building.)*

This application was engineered from the ground up as a **Product-Led Growth (PLG) Machine**:
1. **The Viral Magnet (Resume AI Project Gap Diagnostic)**: Final-year students don't care about generic AI theory; they have placement anxiety. The tool diagnoses their current resume project (e.g., Weather app, basic CRUD) and reveals why recruiters reject it, motivating an instant 1-click registration to fix their score.
2. **Instant Gratification (Digital Admit Pass)**: Generating an official Admit Card with a unique Pass ID (`NXW-AI-XXXX`) turns a boring web form into an official, verifiable placement credential.
3. **Viral Referral Engine ($K > 1$)**: Incorporates a gamified referral loop. Inviting 2 classmates unlocks the *"Top 50 AI Resume Bullets & Interview Cheat Sheet"*, turning every registrant into an active promoter.
4. **Interactive Workshop Sandbox**: Allows students to test the actual application logic (AI Resume Bullet Polisher) right in the browser before attending.
5. **Built-in Evaluator Mode**: A top-bar toggle opens an executive strategy dashboard detailing budget allocation, funnel conversion math, channel schedules, and AI reflection notes.

## 📊 Summary of Strategy Deliverables (Ready for Slides / PDF)

### 1. Student Persona & Hook
* **Who:** Tier-2 and Tier-3 final-year engineering students (CSE/IT/ECE).
* **Burning Pain:** Recruiter hiring freezes and automated ATS filters rejecting resumes with identical generic projects (Todo apps, Netflix clones).
* **The Hook:** *"Build, deploy, and add a live GenAI project to your resume in 60 minutes — with 0 complex math and verified GitHub proof of work."*

### 2. ₹2,000 Budget Breakdown (No Paid Ad Money Burning)
* **₹1,200 (60%):** Top 3 Campus Referral Leaderboard Bounties (1st: ₹600, 2nd: ₹400, 3rd: ₹200 via UPI). Drives 180+ registrations.
* **₹800 (40%):** Micro-bounties for 4 proactive Placement Class Representatives (CRs) at ₹200 each to pin announcements in college batches. Drives 160+ registrations.
* **₹0:** Direct organic WhatsApp / Telegram community seeding (110+ registrations).
* **₹0:** Referral loop unlockables ($K = 0.65$, yielding 90+ registrations).
* **Total Projected Registrations:** **540 (Blended CAC: ₹3.70 per student)**.

### 3. Answers to Mandatory Thinking Questions
* **What changed between your first idea and final solution?**  
  *Pivoted away from spending ₹2k on Meta ads (which yields only ~40 registrations at ₹50 CAC) toward a Product-Led Growth model using referral bounties and interactive resume diagnostics.*
* **If you had another 24 hours, what would you improve?**  
  *Integrate a direct WhatsApp Cloud API webhook to automatically send an Admit Pass image with personalized QR code to students' WhatsApp chat.*
* **What did AI suggest that you deliberately rejected and why?**  
  *Rejected AI's suggestion to reach out to College Principals/HODs via email (bureaucracy takes 3–4 weeks; our campaign is 7 days). Replaced with direct WhatsApp outreach to student Class Representatives (CRs).*
